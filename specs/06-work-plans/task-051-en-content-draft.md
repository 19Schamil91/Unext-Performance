### Wann ist ein Unfallgutachten sinnvoll?

- **More than a minor scratch:** If repair costs, diminution in value or the full extent of the damage are unclear, an accident appraisal provides a reliable basis.
- **Possible hidden damage:** An impact can cause damage that is not immediately visible. The appraisal provides a professional assessment.
- **A clear basis for the insurer:** The appraisal records the extent of the damage, repair costs, photographs and relevant values for the next steps in the claim.

### Freie Gutachterwahl

- Titel: Free choice of appraiser after a non-fault accident
- Text: After a non-fault accident, you can generally choose an independent appraiser yourself. Before you commission us, we will discuss whether an accident appraisal is appropriate in your case.

### Inhalt des Gutachtens

- Einleitung: An accident appraisal records the vehicle damage clearly and professionally. Its exact scope depends on the vehicle, the damage and the findings from the inspection.
- **Repair costs:** Required work, replacement parts, paintwork and other identifiable repair items.
- **Extent of damage:** Visible damage, possible hidden damage, previous damage and vehicle-specific details.
- **Diminution in value:** A possible loss in market value despite proper repairs.
- **Replacement and residual value:** Important values for assessing whether a repair is economically reasonable.
- **Photo documentation:** Clear photographs and a description of the damage for further coordination.

### Hilfreiche Unterlagen

- Einleitung: If available, a few details can help with the initial assessment. We will agree the appropriate way to send them with you directly.
- **Vehicle registration document:** Registration certificate Part I or the main vehicle details.
- **Damage photos:** Existing photographs that may help with the initial assessment.
- **Accident and insurance details:** Details of the other party, insurer or reference number, if already available.
- **Brief description of the damage:** What happened, which areas are affected and whether the vehicle is still roadworthy.

### Ablauf

1. **Contact and initial assessment:** Call, write via WhatsApp or send a short inquiry. We assess the situation initially and clarify the next steps.
2. **Arrange the inspection:** We check what information is already available and how the vehicle inspection can be organised.
3. **Document the damage:** An appraiser inspects, photographs and assesses the damage professionally.
4. **Receive the appraisal:** You receive a clear basis for further discussions with the insurer, repair shop or lawyer.

### FAQ

- **Who pays for the accident appraisal?** If you were not at fault, the other party's liability insurer will generally cover the appraisal costs. For very minor damage, unclear liability or special circumstances, an initial assessment may be useful. We discuss your case before you commission us.
- **Is a repair estimate enough?** A repair estimate may be sufficient for minor damage. An independent appraisal can be useful if the extent of the damage, diminution in value, hidden damage or the claims process is unclear.
- **Can I send photos in advance?** Yes. Existing damage photos help with the initial assessment. We will agree the appropriate way to send them with you personally.
- **Does the vehicle have to be roadworthy?** Not necessarily. Tell us whether the vehicle can still be driven and where it is located. We will then agree the appropriate process.
- **How quickly will I receive a response?** We will contact you promptly after your inquiry to discuss the next steps personally.
